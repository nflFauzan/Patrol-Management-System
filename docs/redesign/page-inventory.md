## 1. Inventory Summary

Across the two frontend applications (`apps/web-admin` and `apps/pwa-field`), a total of **40 route entry points** exist:
- **Web Admin Portal & Command Center**: 26 routes (Next.js App Router).
- **PWA Field Operational App**: 14 functional screens with both modern aliases and legacy PHP filenames for zero-breakage bookmark and QR compatibility.
